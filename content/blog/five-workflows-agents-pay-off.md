After mapping dozens of workflows, the same patterns keep surfacing. Agents pay off fastest when the work is:

1. **High-volume and repetitive** — document intake, triage, and routing.
2. **Multi-system** — tasks that mean logging into three tools to copy data between four screens.
3. **Rule-heavy with edge cases** — where the rules are written down but humans still spend hours applying them.
4. **Latency-tolerant** — the answer is needed in minutes or hours, not milliseconds.
5. **Measurable** — there's already a number attached: cycle time, error rate, first-fix rate.

## What we avoid

Low-volume, high-stakes, single-system tasks with no feedback loop. If nobody can tell whether the output was good, an agent can't either — and neither can its evals.

Start where the math is obvious. Expand where trust is earned.
